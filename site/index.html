How we pick these jobs

Every morning a robot checks the official job boards of companies hiring in India and keeps only openings a student or fresher can apply for.

  • ✓
    Straight from the companyEvery job comes from the company's own careers page, and the Apply button takes you there.

    Made for freshersOnly internships, trainee roles and jobs open to freshers (0–1 years) are shown.
  • ✓
    No pay-to-applyPosts that ask you to pay money are removed. A real company never charges you to apply.
  • ✓
    Fresh every dayThe list is checked every morning; closed jobs disappear.
